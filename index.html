About Our Ministry

ፈለገሕይወት መዝሙር ክፍል (The River of Life Choir Section) is the Sunday School choir program dedicated to raising a generation of worshippers rooted in sacred Ethiopian Orthodox tradition.

Through weekly rehearsals, structured learning, and joyful participation in church services, our students grow in faith, music, and community. We serve across 7 classes from beginners to advanced youth groups.

This digital platform helps our admin team stay organized so that more time can be devoted to what matters most — nurturing the next generation of church musicians.
